7. IMPORTANT SCIENTIFIC LIMITATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The dataset contains 116 samples and is class-imbalanced (87 ASD and 29 controls).

The machine-learning results therefore require cautious interpretation and should not be treated as clinical diagnostic performance.

The leakage-safe Transformer evaluation produced weak generalization performance. Classical models and the feature-size experiment showed stronger validation performance.

The expression measurements originate from lymphoblastoid cell-line material. Therefore, the observed expression differences should not automatically be interpreted as equivalent to expression changes in human brain tissue.

The identified genes are research candidates and do not establish causality or clinical biomarkers.
