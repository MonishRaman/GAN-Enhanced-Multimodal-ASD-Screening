P24: 4. In-Depth Architectural Analysis of Baseline Models
P25: 4.1 Baseline 1: Logistic Regression
P26: Logistic Regression models the log-odds of ASD as a linear combination of the 96 concatenated multimodal inputs (32 Genetic + 32 Behavioral + 32 Environmental): P(y=1|x) = sigma(w^T x + b).
P27: Without GAN Performance: Accuracy: 91.20%, Precision: 89.46%, Recall: 93.40%, F1: 0.9139, ROC-AUC: 0.9682.
P28: With GAN Performance: Accuracy: 90.56%, Precision: 86.27%, Recall: 96.48%, F1: 0.9109, ROC-AUC: 0.9764.
P29: Analysis & Architectural Limitations: Logistic Regression establishes a flat, static linear decision boundary. While fast and surprisingly competitive due to pre-aligned 32-D latent spaces, it possesses zero capacity to model complex cross-modal feature interactions (e.g., how a specific genetic marker modulates a particular behavioral phenotype). Furthermore, GAN augmentation causes a slight drop in precision but improves clinical sensitivity (Recall) by +3.08%.
P30: 4.2 Baseline 2: Random Forest
P31: Random Forest constructs an ensemble of 100 de-correlated decision trees utilizing Gini impurity split criteria across random feature subsets.
P32: Without GAN Performance: Accuracy: 70.08%, Precision: 64.19%, Recall: 90.84%, Specificity: 49.32%, F1: 0.7522, ROC-AUC: 0.9014.
P33: With GAN Performance: Accuracy: 86.78%, Precision: 81.29%, Recall: 95.56%, Specificity: 78.00%, F1: 0.8785, ROC-AUC: 0.9717.
P34: The Massive GAN Breakthrough (+16.70% Acc): Random Forest is notoriously sensitive to severe class imbalance. In the imbalanced training split (3:1 ASD to Control), individual decision trees consistently favor majority-class splits near the root, starving the minority Control leaves. Without GAN, its specificity collapses to a disastrous 49.32% (misclassifying over half of healthy controls). When GAN balances the cohort to 1:1, Random Forest recovers dramatically: Accuracy jumps by +16.70%, F1 climbs by +0.1263, and Specificity soars from 49.32% to 78.00%. This proves the indispensable utility of Objective 2 GAN augmentation for tree-based tabular models.
P35: 4.3 Baseline 3: Support Vector Machine (SVM-RBF)
P36: SVM with Radial Basis Function kernel projects the 96-D multimodal space into an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space via K(x, x') = exp(-gamma ||x - x'||^2), finding the maximum-margin hyperplane.
P37: Without GAN Performance: Accuracy: 95.32%, Precision: 94.19%, Recall: 96.60%, Specificity: 94.04%, F1: 0.9538, ROC-AUC: 0.9913.
P38: With GAN Performance: Accuracy: 94.96%, Precision: 93.16%, Recall: 97.04%, Specificity: 92.88%, F1: 0.9506, ROC-AUC: 0.9907.
P39: Analysis & Clinical Drawbacks: SVM-RBF exhibits strong classification metrics because the pre-aligned 32-D latent spaces (learned in Objective 1) are already smooth and clustered. However, SVM is completely opaque: it offers no modality-level breakdown, cannot explain which features drove the classification, and cannot calculate patient-specific counterfactual interventions. In clinical practice, a 95% accurate model that cannot explain 'why' is unadoptable.
P40: 4.4 Baseline 4: Extreme Gradient Boosting (XGBoost)
P41: XGBoost sequentially builds shallow gradient boosted trees utilizing exact greedy split finding, second-order Taylor expansions of the loss function, and L1/L2 leaf regularization.
P42: Without GAN Performance: Accuracy: 94.30%, Precision: 93.59%, Recall: 95.12%, F1: 0.9435, ROC-AUC: 0.9844.
P43: With GAN Performance: Accuracy: 94.50%, Precision: 93.24%, Recall: 95.96%, F1: 0.9458, ROC-AUC: 0.9890.
P44: Analysis: XGBoost serves as the strongest non-neural tabular baseline. GAN augmentation improves its performance across all major metrics: Accuracy (+0.20%), F1 (+0.0023), Recall (+0.84%), and ROC-AUC (+0.0046). However, XGBoost treats all 96 dimensions flatly and lacks neural attention gates to dynamically weigh modalities on a per-patient basis.

While its effect on deeply regularized neural networks is targeted at improving sensitivity and balancing gradient flow, its impact on tree-based models like Random Forest is transformative (+16.70% accuracy gain).
