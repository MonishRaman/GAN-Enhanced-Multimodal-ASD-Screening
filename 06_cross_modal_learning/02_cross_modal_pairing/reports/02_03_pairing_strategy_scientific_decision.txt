
STEP 02.3 — CROSS-MODAL PAIRING STRATEGY SELECTION

FINAL SELECTED STRATEGY:
Balanced Class-Conditional Pairing

SCIENTIFIC DECISION:

Balanced class-conditional pairing is selected as the primary
cross-modal pairing methodology.

The strategy supports knowledge-level relationships between
independently learned modality-specific representations.

Patient-level correspondence is not used or assumed.

Positive relationships are constructed using same-class semantic
compatibility.

Negative relationships are constructed using different-class
semantic relationships.

Balanced sampling is required to control modality size differences
and class imbalance.

The selected methodology preserves representation diversity while
avoiding exhaustive Cartesian pair generation.
